About

I'm a mining engineer and Senior Associate at MinEx Associates, where Steve Canby and I give investors and lenders an independent, evidence-traced read on whether anything could kill a mining investment case: invest, invest more, or stop.

Alongside that, I build AI operations for industrial businesses. I designed and run a multi-agent AI operation that researches, builds software and manages outreach across my own ventures, and I've rebuilt a London refrigeration contractor's back office from years of paper records into a single working system.

I've worked on the ground in mining and industry across Nigeria, Afghanistan, the UK, Sweden, Germany, Norway, Finland and Chile, so the systems I build fit how the work actually happens.
